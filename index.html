Turning 136K followers into booked clients

An Arabic-language relationship and emotional wellness coach selling sessions, workshops and a course to women in the Gulf and the US. The audience was already big. The problem was conversion.

What I found

  • 500K monthly views but engagement of only ~0.2–0.3%.
  • Under 2% of profile visitors clicked the bio link.
  • Previous sales campaigns spent ~926 AED with no tracked conversions, and reached the wrong audience.

What I did

  • Wrote a strategy report for the client: SWOT, funnel, KPIs and a 5-post pilot.
  • Split 3,728 CRM contacts into buyers, high-intent leads and an exclusion list for Meta targeting.
  • Rebuilt campaigns across UAE, KSA and USA.
  • Set up ManyChat sales flows and a playbook for the DM sales team.
  • Delivered the workshop deck, worksheets, landing page and client community group.

Results

  • 2.08M views and 838 leads in April 2026.
  • 19.7K new followers that month.
  • Cheaper, better-targeted traffic from the rebuilt campaigns.
  • Monthly reports to the client and team.
